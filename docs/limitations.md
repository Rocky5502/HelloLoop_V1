# Limitations

- One model snapshot and one provider route were evaluated.
- Tasks are repository-level Python issues; results need not transfer to other languages or domains.
- Provider, model alias, dependency, repository, and evaluator-image drift can change outcomes.
- Public benchmarks may be present in model training data.
- The eight uncertainty values are engineered proxies, not calibrated probabilities.
- Thresholds were fixed engineering choices, not statistically optimized thresholds.
- Full-vector and intervention diagnostics are post-hoc analyses of fixed trajectories.
- Intervention-triggered subsets are selected by the controller and do not support causal claims.
- Registered and evaluated-only denominators answer different questions; both must be reported.
- A nonsignificant comparison does not prove equivalence or absence of an effect.
- Results do not establish autonomous-agent safety or justify unattended deployment.
- Raw trajectories and patches are excluded for privacy, security, and third-party-content reasons.
